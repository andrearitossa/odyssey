# Experience decisions

Reviewed against the motto: simple and sexy. Research and proposals are inputs, not proof of retention improvement.

## Accepted and commissioned

- **One discovery/continue feed:** the Sol frontend agent replaced the large hero and separate sections with compact cards. The most recent story leads the same feed with a Continue action. The coordinator added its last locally saved scene as a preview; world premise is the fallback on another device.
- **Compact scenes and controls:** 45–75 words of narrative, two paragraphs, three short action choices, custom input always available. Immediate pending feedback and a truthful long-wait state. This word range is a product constraint, not a universal research result.
- **Grounded player agency:** the Sol backend agent tightened the narrator after Terra observed invented objects/people. Unsupported premises should become plausible attempts within the established location.
- **Specific stakes and consequences:** establish a reachable objective and a reason to care; show a usable discovery or lasting consequence after an action. Cut generic promises of danger and redundant questions before choices.
- **Reliable return:** server-saved turns, retry receipts, user-scoped local storage, optional account credentials, private new worlds, and serialized story turns.

## Deferred to preserve simplicity

- A separate structured state-delta protocol or another planning/model call. Keep bounded context and the opening goal for now; long adventures still need continuity evaluation.
- Choice subtitles predicting exact outcomes. Different approaches should be legible from scene and action, preserving discovery and compact cards.
- A new recommendation engine, infinite-scroll mechanics, streaks, or notifications. None has evidence of benefit for this app yet.
- Client engagement telemetry. Before claiming retention improvement, define data handling and measure first-action completion, turn continuation, failed waits, and return with real users.
